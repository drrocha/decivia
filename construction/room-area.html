How it works

Floor area is length times width. The raw box count divides area by the flooring's coverage per box. The waste-adjusted count multiplies that by (1 + waste % ÷ 100), then rounds up, since partial boxes usually can't be purchased.

Worked example

A 12 ft × 10 ft room is 120 sq ft. At 20 sq ft of flooring per box with 10% waste: 120 ÷ 20 = 6 boxes, × 1.10 = 6.6, rounded up to 7 boxes.

Assumptions

  • The room is treated as a simple rectangle; split irregular rooms into rectangles and add the areas.
  • Box coverage is a typical figure — check your specific product's labeled coverage.
  • Any cost shown uses the price you enter; it is not a live market price.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate room floor area?

Multiply length by width in feet. For irregular rooms, split the space into rectangles and add the areas.

How much extra flooring should I buy for waste?

Commonly 10% for simple rooms and 15-20% for complex cuts or diagonal patterns. Always round up to a whole box.

Is the cost estimate here accurate?

No — it is based on the price you enter and is not a current market quote.
