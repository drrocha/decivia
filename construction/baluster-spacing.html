Verify local rules. Building codes can measure openings differently and may vary by railing location. Confirm the permitted clear opening and measuring points before construction.

Clear opening formula

For n balusters and n + 1 equal gaps, clear gap = (run − n × baluster width) ÷ (n + 1). The planner finds the smallest integer n whose opening is no greater than the selected maximum, unless you enter a fixed count.

Worked example

For a 72-unit run, 1.5-unit balusters, and a 4-unit maximum opening, 15 balusters leave an approximately 3.66-unit clear opening. That is not the same as the center-to-center pitch.
