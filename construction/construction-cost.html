Construction cost formula

Material subtotal = quantity × material rate. Labor subtotal = labor quantity × labor rate. Waste allowance = material subtotal × waste percentage. The total adds labor, materials, waste, fees, tax, and contingency.

Worked example

A 400-unit deck at $8 material per unit, 24 labor hours at $45 per hour, and 10% waste gives $3,520 before fees, tax, and contingency; the default 10% contingency produces a $3,872 estimate.

Assumptions

  • Currency is displayed as dollars for convenience.
  • All rates, fees, tax, and contingency are user-provided estimates.
  • Actual bids vary by location, access, scope, schedule, and contractor.
