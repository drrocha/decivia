Charging-Time Calculator

Estimate general battery charge time from capacity, charger current, starting state of charge, target state of charge, and efficiency. This is not an EV charger database.

—

Calculation detail—
Advanced energy and taper considerations

For an energy-based estimate, capacity in watt-hours divided by charger power in watts gives ideal hours. Real constant-current/constant-voltage charging tapers near the target; battery voltage, chemistry, thermal limits, charger protocol, and a taper factor can all make actual time longer.

Charging-time formula

Required capacity = battery capacity × (target SOC − starting SOC). Estimated time = required capacity ÷ charger current ÷ efficiency, where efficiency is a decimal. The page converts percentage efficiency to that decimal internally.

Worked example

A 10 Ah battery moving from 20% to 80% requires 6 Ah. At 2 A and 85% efficiency, the estimate is about 3.53 hours, or 3 hours 32 minutes.

Assumptions and safety

  • Capacity and current use matching units: Ah with A, or mAh with mA.
  • The model assumes a steady effective current and does not model thermal cutback.
  • Use the battery and charger manufacturer's limits; do not use this estimate as a charging safety control.
