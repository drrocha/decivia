How it works

Total cost is the number of pets times visits or nights times the rate per visit or night, plus any surcharge:

Total = pets × visits × rate + surcharge

Worked example

2 pets, 10 visits, $25 per visit, no surcharge: 2 × 10 × $25 = $500. Adding a $30 holiday surcharge brings the total to $530.

Assumptions

  • The rate per visit/night is assumed flat across all pets unless your sitter charges a different per-pet rate (adjust the rate field to match).
  • Surcharge is treated as a single flat addition, not a per-visit or per-pet multiplier.

Reference

Pricing varies widely by sitter and region — confirm the exact rate structure with your pet sitter directly.

Frequently asked questions

How is pet-sitting cost calculated?

Pets × visits/nights × rate per visit/night, plus any surcharge you enter.

Do sitters usually charge extra per additional pet?

Many do, though some use a flat household rate instead. Check your sitter's pricing and adjust the rate field.

What counts as a medication surcharge?

An extra flat fee for administering medication, special-needs care, or holiday-period premiums.
