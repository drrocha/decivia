Scenario comparison

How it works

Monthly total is electricity + gas + water + internet + other. Annual total is the monthly total × 12. Low and high scenarios apply ±15% to model typical seasonal variation (such as higher heating or cooling bills).

Worked example

$120 electricity + $40 gas + $35 water + $60 internet + $0 other = $255/month, or $3,060/year. A low season might run closer to $217/month, and a high season closer to $293/month.

Assumptions

  • Figures are not location-specific unless you enter your own local rates and bills.
  • The ±15% scenario range is a simple model, not a forecast based on your specific utility's rate history.

Reference

For current rates, consult your utility provider's published tariff or your most recent bill.

Frequently asked questions

Are these utility cost estimates specific to my location?

No — they're model-based estimates using the amounts you enter, not live local tariff data.

Why does the estimate show a low and high scenario?

Utility costs vary seasonally, so a ±15% range models that variation around your base estimate.

What should I include in "other" costs?

Any recurring cost you want tracked alongside utilities, like trash service or a heating-oil budget.
