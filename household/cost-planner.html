The combined total simply adds all three figures together for a single reference number — it is not a single monthly cost, since utilities are monthly, lease overage is a one-time projected charge, and pet-sitting is a total for the visits entered.

How it works

Monthly utilities total is electricity + gas + water + internet + other. Lease mileage overage projects your odometer forward (current + monthly driving × remaining months), compares it to your total allowance, and multiplies any excess by your overage rate. Pet-sitting total is pets × visits × rate, plus any surcharge. The combined total adds all three — useful as a single reference figure, even though each section covers a different time period.

Worked example

$255/month in utilities, an 18-month lease projected to run 3,600 miles over its 36,000 mi allowance at $0.25/mi (a $900 projected overage), and 2 pets for 10 visits at $25/visit ($500): the combined total is $255 + $900 + $500 = $1,655.

Assumptions

  • Each section is independent — figures are not shared between sections.
  • The combined total is an additive convenience figure across different time periods, not a normalized monthly cost.
  • All figures are user-entered or model-based estimates, not live local rates or guaranteed pricing.

Reference

For exact utility rates, consult your utility provider; for lease terms, consult your lease agreement; for pet-sitting rates, confirm directly with your sitter.

Frequently asked questions

What does the combined cost planner include?

Monthly utilities, a projected car lease mileage overage, and a pet-sitting total — three independent estimates plus a combined total.

Do the three sections share the same time period?

No — utilities are monthly, lease overage is a one-time projected charge, and pet-sitting is a total for the visits entered. The combined total just adds them together.

Can I skip a section I don't need?

Yes — each section calculates independently, and the combined total only includes sections you've filled in.
